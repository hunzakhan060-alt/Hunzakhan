( Services )

Website development built to actually work, not just launch.

A demo that looks fine on a laptop in a meeting is not the same thing as a site that holds up in the real world — slow connections, older phones, a form somebody actually tries to submit at 11pm. This is the difference between the two.

Start a Project See the Work
( The Problem )

Most site problems aren't visible until real traffic shows up.

A site can look completely finished and still be quietly broken — a contact form that silently fails, a checkout that times out on a slow connection, a page that takes six seconds to load on a mid-range phone. None of that shows up in a quick look during a demo. All of it shows up the first week real visitors arrive.

( What This Costs )

A slow or fragile site is a compounding cost, not a one-time flaw.

Page speed is a direct ranking factor — a slow site quietly loses search visibility every month it stays slow. A fragile codebase turns every future change into a bigger, more expensive job than it should be. And a broken form or checkout doesn't just lose one visitor; it loses every visitor who hits it until someone notices.

( FAQ )

Questions worth answering upfront.

How do you decide between WordPress, Shopify, and custom development?

Based on what the site actually needs to do. Shopify fits stores that need to sell fast. WordPress fits content-heavy sites that need easy editing. Custom development fits projects where neither platform covers what's needed — usually decided during discovery, not before.

Can you work from an existing design, or does it have to be designed here first?

Either. Development-only engagements are available for clients who already have a design from another designer — the build follows whatever's been handed off.

Will I be able to update the site myself after launch?

Yes, for anything content-related. WordPress and Shopify builds include basic training so routine updates don't require a developer. Structural changes still do, the same as with any platform.

What happens if something breaks after launch?

Every project includes a short post-launch window for fixes at no extra cost. After that, maintenance support is available on request without needing a new project scope.

Do you handle hosting and domain setup?

Setup and configuration, yes — hosting and domain ownership stay in your name, not tied to this studio, so there's never a dependency on keeping the relationship active just to keep the site online.
